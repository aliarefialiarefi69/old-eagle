About Old Eagle

Old Eagle (OEAG) is a community-driven project built on the TON blockchain. The project aims to develop an ecosystem around digital assets, NFTs, community activities and future Web3 utilities.

Community

Building an active community around OEAG through events, campaigns and future community initiatives.

NFT Ecosystem

Exploring NFT-related experiences and future connections between OEAG and digital collectibles.

Future Utility

Future utilities and ecosystem features may be developed as the project grows.
