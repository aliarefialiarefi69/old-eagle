1. Introduction

Old Eagle (OEAG) is a community-driven digital asset built on the TON blockchain. The project aims to create an ecosystem connecting digital assets, NFTs, community activities and future Web3 utilities.

The project focuses on transparency, a fixed token supply and gradual ecosystem development.
