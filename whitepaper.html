1. Introduction

Old Eagle (OEAG) is a community-driven digital token built on the TON blockchain. The project aims to create a transparent and accessible ecosystem connecting community participation, digital assets, NFTs and future blockchain-based services.

2. Token Information

Old Eagle is a Jetton token deployed on the TON blockchain. The total supply is fixed at 1,000,000,000 OEAG with 9 decimals.

3. Fixed Supply and Ownership

The total supply of OEAG is fixed. Token ownership has been revoked, meaning the token administrator has been removed. This is intended to provide greater transparency regarding the token contract and prevent future administrative changes to the token supply.

4. Liquidity and Trading

OEAG has an initial liquidity pool on STON.fi consisting of 1 TON/Gram and 10,000,000 OEAG. Trading availability and liquidity may change over time.

5. Planned Utility

The Old Eagle ecosystem is planned to expand gradually. Potential future uses of OEAG may include:

  • Community rewards
  • NFT-related activities
  • Digital services
  • Community campaigns
  • Future partnerships

Future utilities are subject to development, community interest and technical feasibility.

6. NFT Ecosystem

Old Eagle is intended to connect with a growing NFT ecosystem. The project may support NFT creation, community events and future opportunities to use OEAG in connection with digital collectibles.
