🦅 Old Eagle

OEAG — Whitepaper 1.0

Community-Driven Token on TON

1. Introduction

Old Eagle (OEAG) is a community-driven digital token built on the TON blockchain. The project aims to develop an ecosystem connecting digital assets, NFTs, community activities and future Web3 applications.

Old Eagle is designed around a fixed total supply, transparent on-chain information and gradual ecosystem development.

4. Fixed Supply & Ownership

The total supply of Old Eagle is 1 billion OEAG.

Ownership and administrative control have been revoked. This information is intended to make the token's administrative status transparent to the community.

5. Liquidity & Trading

OEAG has an initial liquidity pool on STON.fi.

The initial pool was created with:

  • 1 TON / Gram
  • 10,000,000 OEAG

Because the initial liquidity is limited, trades may experience significant price impact. The market price is determined by trading activity and available liquidity. No fixed or guaranteed price is promised.

  • Establish fixed supply

  • Submit OEAG for wallet ecosystem review

Phase 2 — Community

  • Build social media presence
  • Publish educational content

  • Grow the community

Phase 3 — Utility

  • Develop NFT-related applications

Phase 4 — Ecosystem
