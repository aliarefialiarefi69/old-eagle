4. Fixed Supply & Ownership

The total supply is fixed at 1,000,000,000 OEAG. Token ownership has been revoked, meaning no additional OEAG can be minted through the token owner's authority.

5. Liquidity & Trading

OEAG may be traded through decentralized markets that support the token. Market price can be affected by supply and demand, trading volume, available liquidity and overall market conditions.

There is no fixed price and no guarantee that the market price of OEAG will increase.

6. Planned Utility

The Old Eagle project may develop future utilities around community participation, digital assets, NFTs, campaigns, events and other Web3-oriented activities.

These are development plans and should not be interpreted as guaranteed future features.

7. NFT Ecosystem

NFTs are an important part of the planned Old Eagle ecosystem. Future development may connect OEAG with digital collectibles, community activities and NFT-related experiences.
