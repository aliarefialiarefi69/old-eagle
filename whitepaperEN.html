OEAG — Whitepaper 1.0

Community-Driven Token on TON

1. Introduction

Old Eagle (OEAG) is a community-driven digital token built on the TON blockchain. The project aims to develop an ecosystem connecting digital assets, NFTs, community activities and future Web3 applications.

Old Eagle is designed around a fixed total supply, transparent on-chain information and gradual ecosystem development.

The total supply of Old Eagle is 1 billion OEAG.
